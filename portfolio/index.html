Reinforcement Learning Jan – Mar 2026

Vendée Globe RL

A PPO reinforcement learning agent that autonomously navigates a simulated IMOCA 60 sailboat around the world, using real ERA5 wind data from ECMWF. Trained on two historical Vendée Globe seasons (2016–17, 2020–21) and tested on the unseen 2024–25 edition. Achieved an 80% completion rate with an average race duration of ~85 days, successfully rounding all three great capes.

  • Custom Gymnasium environment with trilinear ERA5 wind interpolation
  • IMOCA 60 polar model for realistic boat speed calculation
  • Interactive 3D globe trajectory visualization (Plotly)
Stable-Baselines3 · Gymnasium · ERA5

Computer Vision · Medical AI Apr – Jun 2026

Retinal Image Super-Resolution

Comparative study of three super-resolution paradigms applied to medical retinal images across four degradation scenarios. Methods evaluated: classical optimization (FISTA with Wavelet L₁ regularization), deep learning (Hybrid Attention U-Net), and generative AI (DiffPIR — Diffusion Plug-and-Play). Benchmarked using PSNR and SSIM metrics.

  • FISTA iterative solver with multi-level wavelet sparsity regularization
  • U-Net with channel and spatial attention mechanisms
  • DiffPIR: score-based diffusion model as plug-and-play denoiser

NLP · Bachelor's Thesis Jun – Oct 2025

CAST — Cinematic Analysis & Screenplay Transformer

Bachelor's thesis project supervised by Prof. Angelo Di Iorio (UniBO). An automated pipeline that converts film screenplays from PDF and HTML sources into TEI-XML, then performs semantic analysis of locations, environments, and temporal patterns across scenes. Includes an interactive Streamlit dashboard applied to a corpus of 100 films and 15,000+ scenes.

  • Multi-source extraction: PDF (pdfminer.six) and HTML (BeautifulSoup4)
  • TEI-XML P5 standard encoding for structured screenplay representation
  • WordNet (NLTK) semantic classification of scene environments
  • Multi-film statistical comparison dashboard (Plotly / Streamlit)
NLTK · pdfminer · BeautifulSoup · Streamlit · Plotly · TEI-XML
